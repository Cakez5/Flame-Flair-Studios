Flame Flair Studios is currently focused on one flagship title, built around fantasy, deception, and difficult choices.

Elementallia: Land of Deception

Elementallia is a JRPG inspired by classic Tales of adventures, following Dreagon on a journey to defeat the Darkness. It centers on fractured trust, dangerous alliances, and a world where only one person can wield an element at a time.
