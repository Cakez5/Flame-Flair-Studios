Development Focus

Roadmap for Elementallia: Land of Deception and the studio.

This roadmap is now phase-based instead of date-based. It shows what the team is building toward without locking the studio into public calendar promises.

Current production phases

  • Core worldbuilding, lore refinement, and faction identity
  • Combat and progression systems iteration
  • Playable vertical slice and atmosphere polish
  • Story flow testing, feedback integration, and feature tightening

What comes after that

Once the core slice feels right, the roadmap shifts toward expansion, refinement, and preparing the game for a broader public-facing reveal. The point is to show direction without binding the studio to specific dates.
