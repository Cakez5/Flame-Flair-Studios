Who We Are

Two creators, one bold vision.

Flame Flair Studios is a small creative team building fantasy worlds with strong atmosphere, meaningful choice, and a focus on story-driven game design.

Founder

Zakary

Lead Developer & Studio Founder

Zakary, the founder and lead developer of Flame Flair Studios, began Elementallia as a book in 2020. Since then, he has dedicated countless hours to bringing the world to life through code, design, and storytelling. In his downtime, he enjoys fishing, volleyball, and sharpening his coding skills.

Art Direction

Mckay

Lead Art Director

Mckay is responsible for the visual identity of the studio, shaping the world’s art direction, character design language, and the fantasy atmosphere players immediately connect with. His work sets the emotional tone and visual clarity for each project.

Our Studio

Built for atmosphere, story, and player connection.

We believe games should feel handcrafted and personal. Flame Flair Studios focuses on creating worlds that are rich in tone, built with care, and designed to leave players thinking about the journey long after the experience ends.
